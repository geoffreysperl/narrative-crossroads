## Why CC BY?

Narrative Crossroads is intended to be freely used, adapted, and shared by educators. The CC BY license ensures:

1. Teachers can modify materials for their classrooms without restriction
2. Researchers can build on the methodology with proper citation
3. The work remains open while maintaining attribution to its origins
