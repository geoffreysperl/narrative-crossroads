## Overview

This session introduces Hamtramck teachers to TTRPG-based pedagogy through hands-on play using *No Thank You, Evil!* — a system designed for accessibility and ease of entry. The focus is on teacher team-building while demonstrating how structured collaborative play works.

### Goals

1. **Team-building** — Teachers experience collaborative problem-solving through play
2. **Methodology introduction** — Plant seeds for potential classroom adoption
3. **Demystification** — Show that TTRPGs don't require gaming expertise
4. **Data gathering** — Collect feedback for refining approach

### Why *No Thank You, Evil!*

- Designed for beginners (including kids), so zero barrier to entry
- Simple mechanics that can be learned in minutes
- Emphasizes collaboration over competition
- Published by Monte Cook Games with educational applications in mind
